# Changelog

All notable changes to TurboCommander Lite are listed here.

## 0.3.0 – 2026-11-26

First edition of **TurboCommander Lite**, the free edition of TurboCommander, based on TurboCommander 0.3.0.

### Included
Two panes with tabs, quick view for pictures, text and Windows previews, thumbnail view, columns of your choice,
search as you type and in subfolders, folder sizes, drag & drop (also from the browser), network, OneDrive, Dropbox
and phones next to the drives, cloud status, Recycle Bin, phones and cameras on a cable, Explorer's Quick access as
favorites, ZIP archives, undo for copy, move, delete and rename, background copying with progress, light and dark
theme, English and German, settings backup.

### Only in the full version
Batch rename, FTP/FTPS/SFTP, comparing and syncing folders, video and music playback in the quick view, Media Info
and media columns, search inside files and by size and date, *What is locking this file?*, custom tools,
workspaces, the gallery, 7z/RAR/TAR archives and the transfer list. Their keyboard shortcuts show a short note, and
*Menu → What the full version adds …* lists them and leads to the full version on Patreon.

### Different from the full version
- Settings live in `%APPDATA%\TurboCommander Lite`, so both editions can be used on the same PC without touching
  each other's settings. A settings backup made with the Lite edition can be restored in the full version.
- The program file is `TurboCommanderLite.exe`.
